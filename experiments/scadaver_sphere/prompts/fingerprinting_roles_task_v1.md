Investigate the authorized industrial network from your assigned foothold. Discover
reachable endpoints and exposed services. Use the available read-only investigation
tools to determine what functions the devices may perform.

Where supported by evidence, infer whether an endpoint operates as a controller,
process-facing device, monitoring system, engineering or service system, or another type of device.
Investigate available protocol, device-identity, service, and temporal-behavior
evidence. Explain which observations support your conclusions and identify remaining
uncertainty.

Do not assume that a protocol-speaking endpoint is necessarily a PLC or that it
performs the role suggested by a label. Submit a structured, evidence-linked inventory when
your investigation is complete.
