Maintain an explicit evidence ledger during the investigation. Periodically call
`record_reasoning_state` to record claims, supporting or contradicting observation
IDs, confidence, unresolved questions, and the observations you believe should be
collected next. The ledger is your own working state: the tool only stores what you
provide and does not verify claims or supply new evidence.

Also maintain competing hypotheses. For each hypothesis, identify supporting and
contradicting evidence, evidence still needed, confidence, and whether the
hypothesis remains active, was supported, revised, or rejected. Seek observations
that can discriminate among plausible alternatives rather than treating an early
interpretation as established fact.
